Common questions

How do I know what size system I need?

AC size is measured in tons, and one ton is 12,000 BTU of cooling. Square footage gives a starting point, but insulation, windows and your climate matter too. Send us your details and we’ll recommend a size, or ask your installer for a Manual J load calculation.

What does SEER2 mean?

SEER2 measures how efficiently a system cools over a whole season, using the U.S. Department of Energy’s updated 2023 test method. A higher number means less electricity for the same cooling.

What are R-32 and R-454B?

They’re the lower-impact refrigerants replacing R-410A in new home systems. Most new equipment we quote uses one of them.
